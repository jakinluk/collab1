# Board

Run `c1 board [--project <id>]` and show the output. Then add, in two or three lines, what most needs attention: blocked tickets, items in "Needs a human", votes this seat hasn't cast, and whether anything is ready to book. Mention that the full live board (with Approve, Resolve and Contest buttons) is at the board URL.
